## The Recommended Setup: Multi-Timeframe Confluence (SMC / Price Action)

Instead of a single indicator, you construct a system built on two distinct layers: The Trend Filter (Higher Timeframe) and The Execution Trigger (Lower Timeframe).

### 1. The Core Architecture

For Day Trading: Map structural zones on the 1-Hour or 4-Hour chart, then execute setups on the 5-Minute or 15-Minute charts.

For Scalping: Map key zones on the 15-Minute or 30-Minute chart, then look for entry confirmations on the 1-Minute or 3-Minute charts.

### 2. The TradingView Indicator Toolkit

To clean up your charts and automate the mapping of these zones, use these highly rated, free community scripts on TradingView:

- Market Structure Features: Search for "Smart Money Concepts [LuxAlgo]". This automatically plots structural breaks (BOS), change of character (CHoCH), and order blocks across your timeframes.
- Volatility & Session Boundaries: Search for "Sessions" (by LuxAlgo or Aura). For Gold and Forex, volume clusters tightly during the London and New York session opens. This indicator visually shades those sessions so you avoid trading during dead or unpredictable market hours.
