Backtest

Replay the 4H trend / 1H structure / 15M trigger engine on MEXC history. Results are approximate: no funding, no partial fills, and stops are assumed to fill at the stop unless the candle gaps through it.

Run a test

Keep the first tests short. 90 days of 15M candles is a useful sanity check.

Fetching history can take 10–30 seconds.

How the simulation works
  • • Entry at the next 15M open after a qualified close
  • • Stop first if a candle hits both stop and target
  • • 2R target, 0.35 ATR structure buffer
  • • Fees and slippage applied to both sides
  • • One position at a time
